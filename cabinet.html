Frequently Asked Questions

How do I post an ad?

Go to "My Ads" and click "+ New Ad". Fill in the category, title, description, price, and photos, then submit. Your ad will be reviewed and go live within a few hours.

How do I top up my balance?

Go to "My Payments", choose a preset amount or enter a custom one, select your card, and click "Top Up". Funds appear instantly.

What is VIP / TOP promotion?

VIP and TOP promotions put your ad at the top of search results and category pages. Both are paid from your wallet balance and renew monthly.

How do I withdraw funds?

Go to "My Payments", switch to the Withdraw tab, enter the amount and your bank details, then click "Withdraw". Processing takes 1–3 business days.

Is posting an ad free?

Yes — standard ads are completely free. You only pay for optional promotions (VIP, TOP) or premium subscription plans.
