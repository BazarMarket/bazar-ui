Frequently Asked Questions

How do I post an ad?

Go to "My Ads" and click "+ Post an Ad". Fill in the category, title, description, price, and upload photos, then submit your listing.

Your ad will be reviewed and usually goes live within a few hours.

How do I promote my listing?

You can promote your listing by selecting one of our paid plans (PRO or VIP).

Promoted listings receive higher visibility and appear at the top of search results.

How do I contact a seller?

Open the listing and use the contact button to send a message directly to the seller.

All communication is handled between users.

Are payments secure?

Yes. All payments are processed securely via trusted payment providers such as Stripe.

We do not store your full card details.

Can I edit my listing?

Yes. Go to "My Ads", select your listing, and click edit.

You can update details, images, and price at any time.

How long does it take to get a response?

We usually respond to support requests within 24–48 hours.

For faster assistance, please include as much detail as possible in your message.

Is Bazar responsible for transactions?

No. Bazar is a marketplace platform that connects buyers and sellers.

We are not involved in transactions and do not guarantee products or services listed by users.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds may be provided in specific cases, such as duplicate payments or technical errors.

Please refer to our Refund Policy for full details.
