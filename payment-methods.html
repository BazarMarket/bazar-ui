Payment Methods

1. Accepted Payment Methods

Bazar accepts payments through secure third-party payment providers, including:

  • Credit and Debit Cards (Visa, Mastercard, etc.)
  • Other payment methods supported by our payment providers

All payments are processed securely via external payment gateways (such as Stripe).

2. Payment Processing

When you make a payment on Bazar, the transaction is handled by our payment provider.

We do not store your full card details on our servers.

3. Currency

All payments are processed in the currency displayed at checkout.

Additional fees may apply depending on your bank or payment provider.

4. Failed Payments

If a payment fails, the service will not be activated.

Please check your payment details or try another payment method.

5. Fraud Prevention

Bazar reserves the right to decline or cancel any transaction if it is suspected to be fraudulent or unauthorized.

6. Responsibility

Bazar is not responsible for any issues arising from payment processing systems operated by third-party providers.
