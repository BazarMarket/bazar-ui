Withdrawals

Users may request withdrawal of available funds from their account balance.

1. Withdrawal Process

To withdraw funds, users must:

  • Go to the "My Payments" section
  • Select the withdrawal option
  • Enter the amount and required payment details
  • Submit the request

2. Processing Time

Withdrawal requests are typically processed within 1–3 business days.

Processing times may vary depending on the payment provider or banking system.

3. Eligibility

Withdrawals are only available for cleared and available balances.

Funds that are pending, under review, or subject to dispute may not be withdrawn.

4. Verification

We may require identity verification before processing withdrawals to prevent fraud and ensure security.

5. Fees

Bazar may apply withdrawal fees where applicable. Any fees will be clearly displayed before confirming the withdrawal.

6. Responsibility

Users are responsible for providing accurate payment details.

Bazar is not responsible for failed transfers caused by incorrect information provided by the user.

7. Right to Hold or Refuse

Bazar reserves the right to delay, hold, or refuse withdrawals in cases of:

  • suspected fraud
  • violation of platform policies
  • legal or regulatory requirements

8. Finality

Once a withdrawal has been processed, it cannot be reversed.
