Advertising Policy

This Advertising Policy sets out the rules for posting listings on Bazar.

1. General Rules

  • Each listing must represent a real product or service
  • Duplicate listings are not allowed
  • Listings must be placed in the correct category
  • Each advertisement should relate to a single item or service

2. Listing Content

Each advertisement must include:

  • A clear and accurate title
  • A detailed description of the product or service
  • Relevant information such as condition, size, specifications, or features
  • Honest disclosure of any defects or issues

3. Images

  • Listings should include real photos of the item
  • Images must not be misleading or taken from other sources
  • The maximum number of images per listing may be limited

4. Prohibited Content

The following are not allowed:

  • Stolen or illegal goods
  • Drugs or drug-related items
  • Weapons, ammunition, or explosives
  • Pornographic or adult content
  • Counterfeit or copyright-infringing items
  • Services or products that violate applicable laws
  • Offensive or discriminatory content

5. Contact Information

  • Users must not include misleading or false contact details
  • External links or redirects may be restricted

6. Moderation

All listings may be subject to review.

Bazar reserves the right to:

  • Edit or remove listings that violate this policy
  • Suspend or restrict accounts for repeated violations

7. Paid Services

Some listings may use paid features such as promotions or highlighted placement.

Use of paid features does not guarantee sale or response.

8. Responsibility

Users are fully responsible for the content of their listings and any transactions made as a result of them.
