Cookie Policy

This Cookie Policy explains how Bazar ("we", "us", "our") uses cookies and similar technologies when you use our website.

By continuing to use our website, you agree to the use of cookies as described in this policy.

1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device (computer, phone, or tablet) when you visit a website. They help the website function properly and improve your experience.

2. How We Use Cookies

We use the following types of cookies:

  • Essential Cookies - These cookies are necessary for the website to function properly (e.g., login, security, basic features).
  • Analytics Cookies - These help us understand how users interact with the platform (e.g., pages visited, time spent, traffic sources).
  • Functional Cookies - These remember your preferences (e.g., language, settings).
  • Advertising Cookies - These may be used by third-party services to show relevant ads based on your interests.

3. Third-Party Cookies

We may use third-party services such as analytics providers or payment processors (e.g., Stripe) that may place cookies on your device.

We do not control these cookies. Please review the respective third-party policies.

4. Managing Cookies

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings.

Please note that disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality of the website.

5. Legal Basis (UK / GDPR)

We use cookies based on your consent where required by law.

Essential cookies may be used without consent as they are necessary for the operation of the website.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time.

Any changes will be posted on this page.

7. Contact

If you have any questions about this Cookie Policy, please contact us through the website.
